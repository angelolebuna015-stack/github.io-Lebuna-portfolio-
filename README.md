## Projects featured

- **Automated Lead Enrichment:** A Zapier workflow that catches a lead through a webhook, enriches it with Apollo, and splits it into high and low priority paths using Google Sheets, Slack, Gmail, and AI by Zapier.

## Run it locally

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.

That's it. No installation needed.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages

1. Push `index.html` to your repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, then select your branch and the `/ (root)` folder.
4. Save, and wait a minute for your site link to appear.

## Customize it

Open `index.html` and look near the top of the `<script>` section:

- `EMAIL` is the email used by the "Copy email" button and the contact form.
- `GH` is the GitHub profile link.
- The `G` list holds the project gallery cards: title, description, tags, and image.
- The `T` list holds the tools.
- The `N` list holds the Thoughts posts.
- The colors and fonts are set in the CSS variables at the top of the `<style>` section.
